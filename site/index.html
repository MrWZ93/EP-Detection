EP Desktop

Review TDMS recordings and mark EP stages.

Local preview. Login and cloud sync are not enabled.

  1. Download

    Download for Windows x64

    Version 1.0.0-preview.2 · ZIP · 31.8 MB · No Python installation

    Open the illustrated training guide →

  2. Extract & open

    Extract the ZIP. Open EP Desktop.exe inside the extracted folder. Keep that folder intact and the launcher window open.

    The program reads local files; your browser displays the review screen.

  3. Choose data

    Get the TDMS data separately from your coordinator. Keep the original folder structure. Click Choose local folder…, enter and save your name, then select a recording.

    Original recordings are read only. Check the acquisition settings before annotating.

  4. Annotate & save

    • Yes: double-click the waveform to add boundaries; drag to adjust. Zoom as needed, then label each region.
    • No: add your reason first, then click No — save and next.
    • Uncertain: retain the file and explain what needs review.

    Click Save locally to save, or Complete and next to finish. Use Export recovery for a backup.

    Saved on this computer only. Nothing is synced to other reviewers.

Where are reviews saved?

Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\EP Desktop\annotations.sqlite3. Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/EP Desktop/annotations.sqlite3.

Blocked by Windows or macOS?

The preview is not code-signed or notarized. Send the prompt to your coordinator; do not disable OS security.
